Frequently Asked

Questions?

A few quick answers about contacting PartsBaba.ai and our RFQ process.

How quickly will I receive a response?
We generally respond to RFQs and enquiries within 24–48 business hours, depending on the requirement and supplier response.
Can you source hard-to-find parts?
Yes. You can share specialised, discontinued, or difficult-to-source requirements with our team. We'll explore suitable sourcing options through our supplier network.
Do I need to know the exact part number?
Not always. If you have a product description, specifications, image, datasheet, or other useful information, share it with us and our team can help identify suitable options.
Is submitting an RFQ an order?
No. An RFQ starts the sourcing and quotation process. An order is confirmed separately after the commercial details are agreed and the required advance payment is received.
Can I contact you about an existing RFQ?
Yes. Please email us at care@partsbaba.com and include your RFQ details so our team can assist you more quickly.
